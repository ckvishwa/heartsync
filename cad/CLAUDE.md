# cad

Enclosure and dock. Fusion 360, driven by `params.csv`. Enclosure work is Phase 10;
until then this folder only holds parameters and notes.

## Source of truth

- **`params.csv` is the source of truth for every dimension.** Change a value
  there, then run `fusion/import_params.py` inside Fusion 360 to update the model's
  user parameters. Never type a dimension straight into a sketch.
- Rows with value `0` and status `verify against datasheet` are unknown. Do not
  guess them: measure the part or read its datasheet.
- **Never hand-edit files in `exports/`.** They are generated from Fusion
  (STL/3MF) and git-ignored. Only release exports are committed, with `git add -f`.

## Hard limits

- **Thickness: 3.5 cm maximum** (`body_thickness_max`). Check the full stack-up
  (fabric, wall, panel, PCB, battery, swelling gap, wall, coil, fabric) before any
  change that adds depth.
- **Battery swelling gap: at least 1 mm (1.2 used)** (`battery_swell_gap`). Nothing sharp
  faces the cell. See `docs/hardware/safety.md`.
- The case must open **without heat**: screws or clips, no glue near the cell.

## Materials (`print-profiles/`)

- PLA: fit tests only. It softens in a warm car or near a charging coil.
- PETG: final enclosure and dock.
- TPU: bumpers and the back ring.
- Profiles are starting points; record what actually worked for the printer used.

## Gasket and IP54 notes

- IP54 is a design target, not a certification.
- Seam gasket channel: `gasket_width`, `gasket_compression`. Keep the channel continuous.
- Mesh over speaker and mic openings; a plug or sealed connector for USB-C.
- Pogo pins and magnets must not break the seal: seat them in blind pockets.
- Do not seal the battery cavity airtight without considering venting.

## Commands

No build step. Validate the CSV by importing it in Fusion; the script reports bad rows.

## Assembly script

- **Ansonia rev G (190 x 76 x 35.5) is `fusion/ansonia_revG.py`**, dimensions in `params_revG.csv`, run through
  `fusion/run_revG.py` (`go("build" | "exports" | "shots")`); boards come from `cad/boards/*.step`
  (`fusion/export_boards.sh`, git-ignored, regenerate). Results and clash list: `docs/hardware/fusion_revG.md`.
  Everything below describes the old 157 mm HeartSync script.
- `fusion/heartsync_assembly.py` builds the whole model from `params.csv` (dimensions) and
  its POS block (part positions). After moving parts by hand in Fusion, run
  `sync_from_fusion()` to write the positions back, then rebuild.
- Bought parts come from `models/` (STEP + datasheet + README per part; read its README).
  No guessed boxes: a part without a published STEP is modelled from its drawing in
  `models/from_drawing.py` and flagged "verify on arrival".
- `fit_check()` reports every clash with volume and overlap box on the open design.
- A full run takes about 5 minutes, longer than the Fusion MCP call timeout: have the runner
  write its report to a file and read that. Import STEP files one at a time in scratch
  documents (four large imports at once crashed Fusion once).
- `cad/net/heartsync_net.dxf/.pdf` (FRONT + TOP fabric), `cad/renders/*.jpg` (via `tools/renders_to_jpg.py`) and
  `cad/exports/*` are generated by it; do not edit. There is no hand-drawn net.

## Back and fabric

- Fabric on the FRONT and the tray's TOP edge, one piece (`cad/net/`); back, bottom and ends are
  bare PETG. The Qi marking is a printed ring 0.4 proud under the front fabric.
- The back is bare PETG with a continuous TPU ring (own STL) in a 1.0 mm channel, over the
  four low-head screws.
- Lid 3.0 mm: the ring channel (1.0 from the back) and the gasket channel (0.8 from the inside)
  leave exactly 1.2 mm. Do not deepen either without thickening the lid.
- Top edge: one TPU strip over two windows (2 mm rib), five low switches, two USB-C ports (ADR
  0017, 0018); ends: round-hole grilles with trim frames sunk flush, length stays 157.
- Stack 34.9 of 35 at the Qi ring (tray 29.4, ADR 0014): any added depth needs a trade.

## Build stages

- `HS_PHASE` (environment, default 4) rebuilds an earlier stage of the 2026-11-19 pass:
  1 size + front, 2 real parts, 3 safety + print fixes, 4 custom boards (ADR 0016).
- The runner pattern: a short MCP script opens a new document, loads the assembly with importlib,
  runs it and writes the report to a file (the run outlasts the MCP timeout); wait for the file.
