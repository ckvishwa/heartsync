## Notes

- The sensor looks out of the board edge (STEP +Y; sensor at X 9.2..16.1, Y up to 17.65), so the board stands at right angles to the front wall and reaches 17.8 mm into the case. That is much bigger than the 10 x 10 stand-in.
- A flat VEML7700 board (option B) suits a front-wall mount better but has no STEP.
